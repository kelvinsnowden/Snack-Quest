The problem

Global products.
Local consumers.
A missing layer in between.

TikTok puts Japanese, Korean, Thai and American products in front of Kenyan consumers every day. The demand is manufactured online. The supply never arrives — and the brands making these products have no efficient way in.

For the consumer

They can see it. They can’t buy it.

No shelf in Nairobi carries what they just watched somebody eat.

For the brand

No route to the African consumer.

No distributor, no demand data, no cheap way to test whether a market exists.

What we do

We make international products
discoverable and accessible.

Live today: a 62-snack catalogue, three curated boxes, M‑Pesa checkout and delivery across Nairobi. Customers choose what goes in the box — and every choice they make tells us what to import next.

The insight

One store concentrates risk.
A network creates data.

The obvious move

One store

One location. One rent. One catchment. One set of assumptions, all of them tested at the same time, with no way to tell which one was wrong.

What we’re doing instead

A Discovery Network

Many locations. Many customer environments. Every one of them returning real demand data before we commit capital to a single address.

I don’t want to raise KSh 8M to open a store. I want to raise KSh 8M to build the system that makes the store replicable.

The business model

Discover → Test → Learn → Source → Distribute.

  • International brands→
  • Import & curate→
  • Demand data→
  • Winning products→
  • Direct supplier terms→

Today we buy at retail arbitrage. Once the data says which products Africa actually wants, those products move to direct brand relationships — better pricing, better supply, and a reason for the brand to talk to us first.

The moat

The machine is hardware.
The network is the asset.

Anyone can buy a machine. What compounds is supplier relationships, location economics, product‑level performance data, the restocking operation, and knowing which products win before spending money scaling them.

How it scales

You own the asset.
