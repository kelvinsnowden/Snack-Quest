Business model

The owner pays for the machine. We earn on it every month.

1

Machine sale one-off

Snack Quest machines, M-Pesa ready and branded, imported and sold to owners.

2

Wholesale snacks every restock

Every machine on the network is stocked by us, from our import line.

3

Management fee monthly

We stock, service and run the machine; the owner sees sales and payouts on a dashboard we have built.

+

Online sales live today

The shop keeps trading and keeps telling us what sells.

The lever is import margin

27.1% gross today, buying locally at close to retail. Importing at volume, co-loaded into an established importer’s containers on Oloitoktok Road, is how we reach the 35% floor and the 45% plan.

Stated carefully

Machine price, management fee and wholesale terms are set with the first contracts. Machines are sold as equipment with a separate service contract. No return is promised to buyers, and the contract goes through legal review, including the Capital Markets Authority question, before the first sale.

Why now

Kenyans already pay with their phones. The machines just need the product.

~53M

Mobile money subscriptions in Kenya, January – March 2026. Subscriptions, not people, but paying a machine by M-Pesa is not a new habit we have to teach.

Machines already sell to Kenyans

Milk and coffee are already sold through vending machines here. The habit exists; a brand people seek out does not.

Where the machines go

  • Airports and transport hubs: JKIA first, with a partner machine already standing there
  • Campuses: USIU first, the audience already watching us on TikTok
  • Offices: the same faces every day, the cleanest read on repeat buying
  • Hospitals and hotels: long waits, visitors, little else on offer

Each placement is footfall somebody else already pays for.

We do not quote a national snack-market figure. Our market is counted in placements and in what each machine sells, numbers the first nine machines will give us. Mobile money: Communications Authority of Kenya statistics as reported by Connecting Africa and Business Today; vending: Africanews, Business Today.
