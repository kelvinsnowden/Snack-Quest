What KSh 8,000 a month has to buy

  • Payment setup and support
  • Remote alerts: sales, stock, temperature, faults
  • Monthly sales report and an owner dashboard
  • Restock scheduling and delivery
  • Service calls coordinated within an agreed time

Not included: stock, spare parts, major repairs, site rent. Proposed scope, to be tested with the first owners.

We will report recurring revenue first: paying machines times the fee, plus gross profit on stock. Machine sales are the way in.

Go-to-market

Prove it on real machines, then sell new ones.

1

Pilot on real machines

Our demo machine proves payments end to end. The nine existing machines (two owners, one at JKIA) test stock, restocking and the fee; their payments depend on each machine’s hardware.

2

Convert the 80

Every machine sold with a management contract, deposit first. The USIU buyer first.

3

Reach 20 paying machines

Airports, campuses, offices, hospitals, hotels, compared on real sales and a 50/50 imported and local mix that changes with the data.

4

Take the winners to shops future

Mini-markets, then supermarkets, with sell-through data in hand.
